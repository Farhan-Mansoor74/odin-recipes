Summer Berry Fool

This summer berry fool is an easy, fresh twist on the beloved British dessert, showcasing the best of summer strawberries, raspberries, and blueberries. Light, creamy, and simple to make, it’s a beautiful way to celebrate berry season.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped ripe strawberries
  • 3/4 cup fresh raspberries
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar, plus more to taste

  2. Meanwhile beat cream and powdered sugar together in a bowl with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form. Keep chilled until ready to serve.
  3. When ready to serve, mash fruit mixture slightly with a fork to break up some of the pieces and thicken the sauce slightly.
  4. Fold about 1/2 cup fruit into whipped cream 2 to 3 times. Add another 1/2 cup to the cream mixture and fold another 2 to 3 times.
  5. Divide cream mixture among serving cups. Top with remaining berry mixture and crumble cookies on top.
